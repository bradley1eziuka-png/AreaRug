The story behind the rugs.

A woman-owned Michigan rug company built on a simple idea: buy from someone who has actually stood on what they sell.

Founded in 2021 by a designer who still sources every collection.

Tina Smart started Area Rug Co. in 2021 as founder and creative director. From the first day, the plan was different from a marketplace: travel to the mills and markets, choose and design the collections in person, and bring them home to Michigan where people could see them in real light.

Tina still travels internationally to source and design original collections. That is why the floor holds thousands of rugs and decor pieces you will not find lined up on every other website, and why the pricing runs honestly from accessible everyday styles to premium natural-fiber pieces.
