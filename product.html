Not sure what size?

The most common mistake is going too small. Watch the two-minute guide, then use the quick rules below for each room.

  • Living room: front legs of every seat on the rug at minimum; all legs on for a more finished look. Most rooms want 8' x 10' or larger.
  • Dining room: leave about 24″ of rug beyond the table on every side, so chairs stay on the rug when pulled out.
  • Bedroom: extend 18-24″ past the sides and foot of the bed for that grounded, warm-underfoot feel.
